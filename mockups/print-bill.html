Choices made on this bill

  • Medicines appear as one line, "Medicines dispensed". Medicine names and symptoms are never printed on a bill.
  • The patient's name and address, and the clinic's details, are fixed as they were on the day of issue.
  • The status box reads ISSUED, PARTIALLY PAID, PAID, VOID or REFUNDED. A void bill prints with VOID across the page.
  • No currency sign and 0% tax for now: both come from clinic settings once the country is decided.
  • A receipt uses the same header with the payment lines only, and can be printed half-page.
