UI prototype

A clickable model of the planned screens, for agreeing the design before it is built for real. Tabs, floating windows, menus, switches and the "View as" role picker work; nothing is saved, sent or printed, and all names and numbers are invented. Best viewed at laptop width (1366 pixels).

Flip through the screens   Use Previous and Next at the top of each screen, or the left and right arrow keys.

The full list of every page, floating window, menu and switch is in ui-inventory.md.
