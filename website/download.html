Installer or portable?

  • Installer adds Jivni to your programs and starts it from the tray. Choose this for the clinic's main computer.
  • Portable runs from any folder and keeps its data beside the program. Unpack it and open Jivni; nothing else is changed on the computer.
  • Either way, the first screen sets up your clinic and shows your recovery key once. Write it down.

Prefer a server?

Jivni also runs in Docker with PostgreSQL, for a clinic that already keeps a small server. The instructions are in the project's README.
